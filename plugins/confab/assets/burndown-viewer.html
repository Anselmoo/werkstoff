Has this cleanup converged?

A confab cycle has converged when every finding it opened is closed and the latest pass had nothing left to close; it has stalled when a pass closes nothing while findings are still open. The exact counts for this run are filled in from the ledger below.

Escalated is not a worse kind of open. It is a finding confab reopened past its thrash limit and refused to retry — the cycle gave up and handed it back to a human, and it will never close on its own.

  • ✓closed — fixed and re-verified; needs nothing
  • ●open — still queued for a later pass
  • ▲escalated — a human must act; the cycle stopped retrying
  • ╱cumulative closed — the trend line, findings closed so far
