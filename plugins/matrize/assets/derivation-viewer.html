Computed contrast

Ratios are computed by scripts/contrast.py and cannot be supplied; the builder rejects input that states one. The chart shows margin, which a table flattens: a pair just short of 4.5 is a near miss fixed by darkening a few percent, and one near 1.0 is a different colour.
