click a node: its ancestors and dependents light up, the rest dims

This is the one interactive artefact matrize emits. Everything else is print-first, because an approval artefact ends as a PDF in front of someone who does not get a localhost URL. A graph earns the exception: it has no readable static layout, and the reader arrives with a target question — why is this token this value, and what breaks if I change it? Click any node and both answers light up at once.
