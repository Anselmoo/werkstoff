Did this review actually finish?

A stage nobody reached is not a stage somebody skipped: a skip is a recorded decision with a reason attached, an unreached stage is an unfinished review.

The nine stages are cupertino's design techniques, run in a fixed order because each decides something the next depends on — which is why a stage nobody reached leaves a real hole rather than a missing formality. A solid green outline is a stage that ran and persisted a result. A dashed amber outline is a stage explicitly skipped — it carries its own recorded reason, visible in the panel on the right. A muted grey stage was never reached, so nothing was decided there at all and no reason exists to read. Outline style says this as well as colour does; the legend at the foot of the page repeats it in words.
