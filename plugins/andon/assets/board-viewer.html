Where is the line stopped, and why?

The line is stopped at the first wire whose proof is red, hung, or absent — this board names that wire and refuses to call the stream converged while any gap behind it is still open.

A wire is one stage's handoff to the next. A wire is green only because a linked evidence doc says so. Amber means verification was attempted and did not settle; a dashed edge means it was never attempted at all — the two are different failures, not degrees of one. Red tagged Tier 1 is the single verdict nobody can override. A tile outlined in red is a number that must reach zero before this stream can converge.
