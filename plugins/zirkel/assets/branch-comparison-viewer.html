Which branch wins, and what does winning cost?

The winner is the branch with the highest raw Total of Feasibility + Impact + Risk; this report names it, its margin over the runner-up, and the blocker that margin has to survive.

Total is the raw sum of Feasibility + Impact + Risk — Risk is never inverted, so a branch can win on Total while carrying the highest risk on the board, and an exact tie on Total goes to the lower Risk score. The winning row is the one with the amber left border and the WINNER tag, never colour alone.
