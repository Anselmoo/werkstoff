# Manifest formats

## Contents
- pyproject.toml
- package.json
- Cargo.toml

## pyproject.toml
Dependencies live under `[project] dependencies` and `[project.optional-dependencies]`.

## package.json
Dependencies live under `dependencies`, `devDependencies`, `peerDependencies`.

## Cargo.toml
Dependencies live under `[dependencies]` and `[dev-dependencies]`.
