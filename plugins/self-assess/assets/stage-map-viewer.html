Which stages can't be changed on their own?

The ones every other stage imports, and the ones that import each other: everything downstream of a god-module has to be re-tested with it, and a dependency cycle cannot be broken from one side.

What the marks mean

  • ordinary stage — thin grey ring
  • god-module — most stages import it (thick amber ring)
  • in a dependency cycle — double red ring, or a red core when small; its number names the cycle
  • dead-end — imports nothing outward (dashed ring)
  • circle area — files in the stage
